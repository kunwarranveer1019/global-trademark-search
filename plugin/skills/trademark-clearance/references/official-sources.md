# Starter official trademark source directory

These links are starting points, not proof that a search was completed. Open the source, enter the candidate and relevant variants, choose relevant goods/services classes, inspect results and record the direct result links. Confirm that each portal is current before relying on it. “World” includes these curated portals plus WIPO country-profile/office-directory links for ISO codes without a configured portal; those links only help locate the national register and do not mean it was searched.

| Code | Jurisdiction / source | Search portal | Coverage and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| WORLD | WIPO Global Brand Database | https://www.wipo.int/en/web/global-brand-database | Multi-collection discovery tool. WIPO advises searching national or regional registers too. |
| EU | EUIPO Search IP / TMview | https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip | EU and participating offices; not every country. |
| US | USPTO Trademark Search | https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/ | US federal trademark records. |
| GB | UK IPO Trade Marks Search | https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ | UK trademark records; offers Nice class filters. |
| CA | Canadian Trademarks Database (CIPO) | https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/ | Canadian records, including active and many inactive marks. |
| AU | Australian Trade Mark Search (IP Australia) | https://search.ipaustralia.gov.au/trademarks/ | Australian registered and pending records. |
| IN | IP India Trade Mark Public Search | https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/ | India; interactive search. |
| JP | J-PlatPat | https://www.j-platpat.inpit.go.jp/t0100 | Japan; include Japanese character and transliteration checks as relevant. |
| BR | INPI Brazil | https://servicos.busca.inpi.gov.br/marcas/ | Brazil; Portuguese-language interface. |
| KR | KIPRIS | https://www.kipris.or.kr/ | South Korea; include Hangul and transliteration checks as relevant. |
| SG | IPOS Digital Hub | https://digitalhub.ipos.gov.sg/ | Singapore Similar Mark Search; interactive portal steps may be required. |
| CN | CNIPA National IP Public Service Platform | https://ggfw.cnipa.gov.cn/ | China; Chinese-language search and manual review may be required. |

Use this table as a starting point and validate the operator, URL, jurisdiction, data scope, and terms before use. Do not infer that an unlinked country has no trademark register. For logo/device marks, check whether that specific official portal supports image, Vienna-code, or figurative-mark search; otherwise state that this part remains unsearched.

# Registry source policy

`sources.json` contains source portals selected for initial coverage. The links are official or institution-run entry points. These sources have different jurisdictional scope and search behavior; the utility creates portal links and supplies the exact query term to enter, but does not automate form submission or scrape results.

Before adding an adapter, verify the source operator, geographic coverage, status/date fields, terms of use, permitted API or download, and authentication requirements. Prefer documented APIs or official exports. Do not evade CAPTCHAs, rate limits, login walls, or technical restrictions. Review the source page and preserve its direct URL in research notes.

Initial configured national portals are US (USPTO), UK (UK IPO), Canada (CIPO), Australia (IP Australia), India (IP India), Japan (J-PlatPat), Brazil (INPI), South Korea (KIPRIS), Singapore (IPOS), and China (CNIPA). Regional/international discovery portals are EUIPO/TMview and WIPO Global Brand Database. This is a starter set, not a comprehensive global directory. WIPO itself advises searching national/regional office registers in addition to its database.

“World” includes only configured links and must never be described as comprehensive global coverage. Aggregated databases can omit national records; search the national register in each target market as well.
